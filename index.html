In this essential walkthrough, Dr. Angela Reed demonstrates the circular palpation technique you can do while showering. Soap reduces friction, enabling you to feel minute changes or firm masses in breast tissue.

🎯 What To Look For During This Check:

  • Use the pads of 3 middle fingers, never just pinch with fingertips
  • Make overlapping coin-sized circles across the entire chest wall
  • Check lymph nodes in armpits for painless firm swelling
  • Note any dimpling, puckering, or fluid secretion
